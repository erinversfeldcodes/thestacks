# US-12.3.4 — LLM Claims Are Checked Against Their Sources

## 1. User Story

> **As a** reader, **I want** the platform to measure whether its model-written claims are
> supported by their sources **so that** generated text earns trust with numbers, not tone.

**Status: substrate exists, measurement does not.** `mart_llm_faithfulness` is currently a
confidence histogram whose consumer was superseded — confidence is the model grading itself,
which is the vocabulary the cascade ruling banned for acceptance decisions.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Faithfulness is judged against SOURCES (the association's book metadata; the assistant's
  retrieved spans), by rubric or judge with its own recorded agreement rate — never by the
  producing model's confidence alone.
- The metric gates promotion for its surface (the W7e lesson: numeric, wired, able to fail).

## 2. Validation (when built)

- A planted unfaithful output scores below the bar and is refused by the gate.
