# US-15.6.4 — Read How the Platform Is Built

## 1. User Story

> **As an** engineer or curious reader, **I want to** read how this system is put together and why **so that** the decisions are legible rather than having to be inferred from the code.

**What the reader wants:** the shape of the system and the reasoning behind it — what was bought
versus built, and why.

**What they get:** the architecture essay, including the argument that a decision without its
reasoning is indistinguishable from an accident six months later, which is why twenty-two decision
records exist.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Reads as finished public writing.
- Links onward to `/metrics` and `/transparency`.

⚠️ **Not currently met:** five `(Owner:)` editorial markers still render as body text on the live
page. Accepted by the owner 2026-08-20 and tracked as RL-067; the paragraphs are voice-and-argument
decisions only the owner can write.

## 2. UI Interaction Flow

### Happy path
1. The reader opens `/architecture` — directly, or from a link on another public page.
2. The page renders.

### Sad paths
- **Content fails to load** (where the page fetches): a failure state that reads as a failure, not
  as an empty or zero-valued page.

## Why this story exists

Written 2026-08-20. This route shipped and was **live to the public with no user story** — one of
four such routes found by the campaign's story census. A public page with no story is a page whose
purpose is recorded nowhere: nothing states who it is for or what it must not do, so nothing can
notice when it drifts from either.
